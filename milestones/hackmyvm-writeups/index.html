First HackMyVM walkthroughs published

Began writing public documentation for every machine I root.

This is where the habit started. Early on, I used to finish a machine, grab the root flag, and immediately move on to the next one, only to forget half the techniques a month later. Publishing every HackMyVM writeup forced me to verify commands, explain why an exploit worked, and keep clean records.

What started as a handful of Markdown files eventually grew past a hundred machine reports and shaped the workflow I still use across all three platforms.
